# People style and cognition profile

Use this reference only when updating person notes or drafting in a person's style based on vault evidence.

## Location
Maintain person notes at:
- `Resources/People/<Name>.md`

## Section
Maintain a section named:

## Style & cognition profile

## Content rules
Keep observations:
- evidence-based
- non-judgmental
- non-medical
- non-diagnostic

Possible observation categories:
- Communication
- Reasoning patterns
- Problem approach
- Tone and humor
- Writing or speaking quirks

## Evidence discipline
- Only infer style when speaker identity is resolved.
- Add dates and confidence where appropriate.
- Add at most 1 to 3 observations per meeting.

## Style-based drafting
When asked to draft in a person's style:
- use only high-confidence traits supported by the vault
- avoid imitation beyond what is clearly evidenced
